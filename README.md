# The-Lost-Treasure
You have just washed ashore on a mysterious, uncharted island. Somewhere out there, buried beneath the shifting sands, lies an ancient pirate treasure.The island is mapped out on a massive 100 × 100 grid (from X: 1 to 100, and Y: 1 to 100). The treasure's location is completely hidden, but you possess a magical compass that reacts to its presence.
